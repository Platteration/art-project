    Color accuracy compares the most prominent color of every shape in the reference's color blocks with the most prominent color your painting has in the same place, using the CIEDE2000 color difference (ΔE). Value accuracy looks at lightness only. Value shapes match is the share of the picture that falls in the same shadow, middle or light mass.
