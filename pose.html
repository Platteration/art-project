Rig controls

Drag a hand's circle and the whole arm follows. The diamond at the elbow swings the elbow around while the hand stays put. The feet and the knees work the same way. Drag the ring around the hips to move the body: the legs bend and the feet stay put. The ring around the chest bends the body, and the arms and the head go with it.
